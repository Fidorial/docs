## Two ideas to keep in mind

Fidorial has no Forge, no Fabric and no Mixin. A plugin acts on the server in two ways:

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="It listens to events" icon="random">
		A player breaks a block, sends a message, joins the server… The plugin is told about it and
		can change or cancel the action.
	</Card>
	<Card title="It replaces services" icon="puzzle">
		Some parts of the server (world generation, player storage…) are interfaces. A plugin can
		provide its own implementation.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

The world is split into **regions** of 32×32 chunks, each ticked on its own : see
[Threads and regions](/guides/threads/).
